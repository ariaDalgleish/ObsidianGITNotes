Ask questions

Research: 
	their main site -> about us | careers
	linkendin -> recent posts

	Understanding them:
		what roles r they hiring and looking for?
		whats their values, mission, and vision?
		What do they do? Who do they serve?
		Do they have graduate or inernship opportunities for entry-level talent? 
	Understanding yourself:
		what interests you about the organisation?
		do you align with their values, culture, and working environments?

Introducing yourself - Elevator Pitch
	Keep it short, succinct (30 - 60 secs)
	Keep conversation relevant 
	Have a question(s) ready
Example: 
	Introduce name and study
	Show your interest - what area that you can initiate conversation - find topic of common interest
	Ask something that your interested to find out - what opportunities, what qualities you're looking for in graduates.
