# Phillips story

1. Phillip gets ready for work, packing his belongings grabbing his prepped meal and kisses his wife before walking out the door.
Feelings: Loved, comfortable, organized, and excited to work hard.
Quotes: "I'll see you soon, have a lovely day dear!" "Today will be fantastic"

2. Before he checks for a ride he reads an email about an urgent call with a client in a couple of hours - which he wasn't expecting.
Feelings: Surprised, off-balance, still confident

3. Phillip want's to get to work as soon as possible and believes Clarity Drive to be reliable and likes the quiet cabin so he can focus on his tasks for the day. So he calls for a ride.
Feelings: Concentrated, decisive

4. He gets in and reads through his work emails, he's not paying attention to his surroundings being blissfully unaware.
Feelings: Focused, distracted.

5. Phillip checks his E.T.A as he's conscious of time and wants to get to work to get prepared for client call. He's on time. 
Feelings: Relieved and comfortable in quiet cabin.
Quotes:
Opportunities for improvement: Display E.T.A on car's interface for easier reading.

6. Unaware he fails to notice road closure, AV fails to notify the disruption and changes route adding 5 mins delay on E.T.A. Phillip notices the unusual change in route and sees his E.T.A delay.
Feelings: Confused and distressed from lack of cue information and public notification of AV decisions. He starts to feel anxious as he doesn't know what the AV system will do.
Quotes:
Opportunities for improvement: Warning to user about obstructions on route, the final decision made, and the impact to the journey.

7. AV suddenly brakes after detecting cyclist in blind spot, causing alarm in Phillip by the sudden action.
Feelings: Alarmed
Quotes:
Opportunities for improvement: Show confidence on decision by announcing the cause of braking and reasoning, the impact to the journey

8. Cyclist then moves into view and Phillip feels calmer seeing the cause of alarm - knowing no one was harmed & AV system made the right decision and acted quickly.
Feelings: Relieved no one is harmed, irritated that there was no warning or explanation followed. He felt out-of-control and distrust with Clarity Drive - no apology or responsibility take afterward to check on user.
Quotes:
Opportunities for improvement: After announcing the event, apologize for users disruption, have open options for user to leave review of experience.

9. On arrival Phillip rushes out of vehicle to his office.
Feelings: Overwhelmed with stressful commute, unhappy with how his morning has started, distracted from his work tasks.
