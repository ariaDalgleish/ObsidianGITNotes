ABCS Framework

## Anthropometrics
The shape of the body and how it influences what is designed;
Consideration of the **physical characteristics** of intended users such as what size they are, muscle strength they have ....

- Physical aspects and interaction with devices
- Ergonomic and risk factors considerations including **posture, load bearing** and **input/output** devices
Examples:
- Workstation design
	- Keyboard, touch, pointing devices, etc
- **Input gloves**
- Objects with RFID tracking tags e.g.,
- Children's toys: 'Feel'/physical nature of the objects and computational aspects
- Immersive devices
- 

## Behaviour:
Perceptual and motivational characteristics, looking at what people can **perceive** and why they do what they do
## Cognition:
Learning, attention, and other aspects of cognition and how these processes influence design; users defined by how they think and what they know and what knowledge they can acquire.
## Social Factors:
How groups of users behave, and how to support them through design; users defined by where they are-their context broadly defined, including their **relationships** to other people.
