# Brief
## Autonomous Vehicle

**Problems**: 
==Drivers not pay attention to driving.==
Often multitasking with social media, music, conversations, screens. 
During everyday commuting in urban journeys like airport transfers or routine errands.

==Cabin environment is quiet.==
Questions to ask: 
- Does this mean it is peaceful? 
- empty? 
- lacking?
- bland? 
- quiet traffic? 
- quiet inside or outside?

What is the problem? The interface? environment? situation? task?
==Human factors== - sleep quality, dieting, health, routine, exercise.

**Result is user attention is intermittent and directed away from interface (surrounding)**.
In critical scenarios, vehicle will alert driver & request manual override of driving control.
Different levels of automation
- 0 - 2 
	- Cruise Control 
	- Monitoring speed
	- The human monitors the driving environment
- 3 - 5
	- The automated system monitors the driving environment

From test result there is a lack of clarity. Surprises drivers, erodes trust, increasing anxiety particularly for motion sensitive users.
> " Why did the car do that? "
> " What will happen next? "

### Brainstorming
If users are distracted most on their phone. Perhaps the automation can be connected through their phone.
If user gets notification - car will override so user can check/act on notification. Same with phone calls.

It would be most useful for long drivers or truck drivers. 
Humans get bored if tasks are repetitive - job related driving being frequent back and forth driving to destinations. Users might feel unproductive, want to use their time/long hours on something more impactful/meaningful like studying.
If user is studying automated driving needs to be seamless, smooth, to minimize distractions, anxiety, or stress about driving. 

Take factor of other users in vehicle other than driver, and users outside of vehicle.

# Fishbone

The 6Ms (Manufacturing and Production)
- **Manpower / Mindpower**: Human errors, training gaps, or skill shortages.
	- **Users** - demographics, attention, physiology 
		- Age diversity
		- Unfamiliarity with AV systems
		- Multitasking - divided attention
		- Motion sensitivity
		- Multiple stakeholders 
			- Other possible users outside and inside 
- **Machine**: Equipment, tools, or technology malfunctions.
	- **Automation system** - What the AV itself does and decides
		- Handoff in level 3 automation.
			- Moment control passes from system to driver. (Often short notice). For example: Emergency brake - detects and reacts before the human is aware there's a situation. 
			  For an override request: the driver is multitasking and suddenly needs to reconstruct situational awareness fast enough to safely resume control.
		- Opaque decision making
		- Hidden hazard detection
		- Sudden braking
- **Material**: Raw parts, defective supplies, or incorrect items.
	- **Information content** - What's actually in the interface notification
		- No duration/impact estimate
			- "No indication of duration or impact forcing users to infer meaning." No data showing - effect on the trip, what problem is this solving? How long will the detour take?
		-  No adaptation in interface to suit user
			- Different demographics - Elder, vision.
- **Method**: Standard processes, procedures, or operational steps.
	- **Interface & communication** - How/when the system talks to the user
		- Vague notification
		- Low salience
			- Distinction between updates/changes.
		- Reactive not Pro-active design
- **Measurement**: Faulty data, incorrect metrics, or calibration errors.
	- **Feedback & trust** - signals (or lack of) that let users gauge outcome/confidence
		- No anticipation cue
		- No trust calibration
		- No confidence signal
		- Trust erosion
		- Anxiety
- **Milieu / Mother Nature**: External environmental conditions like temperature or humidity.
	- **Environment & context** - The cabin, the trip type, external conditions
		- Ambiguous quiet cabin
			- Peaceful? Empty? Bland? - not resolved anywhere in case study of "quiet" meaning
		- Urban trip variety
		- Weather/Traffic/Construction triggers.
