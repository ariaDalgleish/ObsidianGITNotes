
2025 I built a solo winter survival game in Unity as my capstone project.

This game acted as my goal to challenge myself as a solo developer to design an atmospheric survival game by prototyping survival elements and mechanics such as crafting, cooking, and collecting.

When designing my game I spent hours studying the game I was inspired by, looking at the details of how exactly the mechanics worked, the footprints in the ground, the players movement sliding down hills, the timings of decay, how many sounds went into picking up a flower. I watched documentaries from the design team of The Long Dark, hearing the process they took to make the decisions

This project was for my final year of my game design course, I based my design choices on my research question: _How can I design a survival game that uses mechanics to create a sense of isolation that_ _feels comforting rather than 'eerie’?_ I've really enjoyed working on this and will continue to develop the game! Some big plans I have is to actually make 3D models and proper art assets as I only focused on mechanics and game feel before, I also want to design and create a real narrative, and map out the environment to be a story telling game. I'm excited for where I can take this but for now I hope you enjoy my _demo_ survival game. I also had to cut some mechanics out because I ran out of time...
