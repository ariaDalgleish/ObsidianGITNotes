Technology is changing the way we work. "Tell us about something you personally have learned, explored or experimented with recently and how it changed the way you think."


Technology is advancing rapidly and as a computer science student, even before as game developer, I was always pressed with the questions about AI, how has AI changed the way I work, what about my studies, how do I personally feel about AI doing my work for me. 
Even in this moment I felt expected to discuss AI and how I've experimented with the tools that are now available. 

So I'm going to address these questions in my own words. AI hasn't exactly had negative impact on me, it has pushed my drive to learn, my motivation to study and not rush through the steps.

To me, AI ruins all the fun of discovery, motivation, and the rewards of hard work. 

I love to go to the library read up the recommended chapters and resources for my classes, or of course talk to the professionals. Learning the coding principles from the text books of the people before that built the foundation that AI now stands on.
That's exactly why even before my courses started I bought this beautiful book about creating programming languages. 
Why I have hours and hours on YouTube tutorials, I've researched studies, and written essays on these carefully crafted creations. 
When developing my game I spent hours studying the game I was inspired by, looking at the details of how exactly the mechanics worked, the footprints in the ground, the players movement sliding down hills, the timings of decay, how many sounds went into picking up a flower. 

Recently I've seen with advances of VR development how companies are using this software to make a positive impact on peoples lives. For example making training simulators, for people in construction to practice using high risk equipment without endangering the people and environment around them.
Its beautiful seeing the new technology being used in these ways and that's exactly why I study this, so that I can get to the point of creating a positive impact to the users lives through my own developments, studies, and work.
